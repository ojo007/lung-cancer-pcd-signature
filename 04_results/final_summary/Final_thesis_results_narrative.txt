HEADLINE RESULTS

1. PCD catalogue
Result: 296 unique genes across five programmed-cell-death programmes
Interpretation boundary: Curated reference catalogue

2. Concordant PCD candidate set
Result: 47 concordantly dysregulated tumour-normal genes
Interpretation boundary: Not itself a prognostic signature

3. Molecular subtypes
Result: Two expression-derived PCD subtypes retained independently in LUAD and LUSC
Interpretation boundary: Expression-derived groups; not causal entities

4. External subtype validation
Result: Eight independent GEO cohorts showed partial, mechanism-specific reproducibility
Interpretation boundary: External silhouettes were modest

5. Multi-omics characterization
Result: Subtype differences extended across mutation, CNV, methylation, pathways and immune contexture
Interpretation boundary: Multi-layer characterization, not a joint multi-omics integration model

6. LUAD MPCDS internal performance
Result: Repeated nested-CV C-index 0.6043
Interpretation boundary: Modest discrimination

7. LUSC MPCDS feasibility
Result: Repeated nested-CV C-index 0.5051; no defensible LUSC MPCDS
Interpretation boundary: Negative/weak finding retained

8. LUAD external MPCDS validation
Result: Four LUAD GEO cohorts; adverse-risk direction in 4/4 and significant in 3/4
Interpretation boundary: Fixed TCGA weights; no outcome refitting

9. Time-dependent discrimination
Result: External 1/3/5-year AUCs generally above 0.5, with sparse-horizon uncertainty
Interpretation boundary: Interpret sparse follow-up horizons cautiously

10. Decision-curve analysis
Result: Clinical+MPCDS C-index 0.7243 vs 0.6741 clinical-only in TCGA complete cases
Interpretation boundary: Exploratory/apparent development-cohort analysis

11. PRISM pharmacogenomics
Result: 1,440 compounds tested; no FDR-significant associations
Interpretation boundary: Cell-line pharmacogenomic evidence only

12. GDSC2 predicted sensitivity
Result: 110 MPCDS and 104 subtype associations at FDR<0.05; limited PRISM concordance
Interpretation boundary: Transcriptome-derived in-silico prediction, not observed patient response

13. Immunotherapy-related indicators
Result: CD274 and nonsynonymous mutation burden associated with MPCDS; MANTIS was not
Interpretation boundary: Mutation burden is not formal mutations-per-megabase TMB

14. TIDE surrogate analysis
Result: Higher MPCDS associated with higher TIDE; PCD_C2 had more TIDE no-benefits cases
Interpretation boundary: TIDE is an in-silico surrogate, not observed ICI response

15. LASSO sensitivity analysis
Result: Four-gene sparse model showed held-out C-index 0.5242
Interpretation boundary: Sensitivity analysis only; does not replace primary ridge model

16. Immune deconvolution sensitivity
Result: Five complementary deconvolution methods identified replicated and method-dependent immune signals
Interpretation boundary: Cross-method agreement prioritized over any single method

17. Integrative PCA/Sankey
Result: 47-gene PCA plus 496-patient subtype-MPCDS-stage Sankey dataset
Interpretation boundary: Descriptive integrative visualization
